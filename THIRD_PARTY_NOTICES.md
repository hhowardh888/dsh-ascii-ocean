# Third-party notices

This bundle adapts the Canvas simulation and ASCII ink renderer from
[ASCII Sharks Wallpaper](https://github.com/s1egg/ascii-sharks-wallpaper), which is
itself adapted from [stackedlol/ascii-sharks](https://github.com/stackedlol/ascii-sharks).

- Adapted files: `src/world.ts`, `src/renderer.ts`, `src/motion.ts`, `src/shapes.ts`,
  `src/species.ts`, `src/math.ts`, `src/interaction.ts` and the frame loop in
  `src/engine.ts`.
- Changes for Harness: transparent ink-only canvas, theme-derived ink palette,
  DOM-measured feathered reading veil, and a mount that places the ocean behind the
  conversation.
